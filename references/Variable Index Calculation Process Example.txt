Variable Index Calculation Process Example 

1. Sea-level rise 
* Data was obtained from sea-level rise viewer
* 1-, 3-, 5- and 7-feet scenarios 
* Data was obtained as a vector polygon
* Literature suggests a classification of 0 as low (1), 3-5 feet as medium (5) and 6 or higher as high (10). 
2. Income 
* Data was obtained from census bureau at block level 
* Data was reclassified using literature
* Data was in table form 
* A join was done with census block shapefile
* Literature suggests a classification of $20,000 or less as high (10), $20,000-50,000  as medium (5) and at or above $50,000 as low risk (1).
